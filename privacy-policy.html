This Privacy Policy explains how WhiteFlows International collects, uses, stores, and protects information submitted through whiteflowsint.com. It applies to consultation forms, proposal submissions, direct communications, and website usage data.

Last Updated: April 27, 2026

We may collect identity details, contact information, location details, investment preferences, funding requirements, proposal details, and any documents or information you voluntarily submit during an enquiry or application process.

How We Use Information

Form submissions are transmitted over HTTPS and may be processed through website infrastructure, secure email delivery providers, and internal communication workflows used by WhiteFlows International. We may use cookies or similar technologies for session handling, interface preferences, traffic analysis, and website performance. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, though some website features may work differently if cookies are disabled.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary with trusted service providers, advisors, operational partners, or legal and regulatory authorities in connection with legitimate business processing, compliance obligations, or rights protection.

Retention and Security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for advisory evaluation, client servicing, documentation, internal recordkeeping, or compliance purposes. We use commercially reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage workflow can be guaranteed to be absolutely risk-free.

Your Choices
